# BizzBee outreach messages: working rules

Rules agreed with the team for writing client Outreach Messages documents.

## Sources to read first
- The client's folder on Drive (ICP research, onboarding form, LinkedIn profile optimisation).
- Recent Outreach Messages docs in other client folders, for structure and style.
- Sweet Leads (Dancho Dimkov), Copy chapter: duality, empathy, credibility, free value.

## Template
- BizzBee template (header, footer, standard intro, The Solution, Target Audience, LinkedIn messages, Email messages).
- Messages in boxed paragraphs, placeholders in bold orange, grey "Response suggestion" notes.

## Writing
- Human, natural, simple, clear, concise. Each message must fit every company in that ICP.
- Never use AI-sounding words, e.g. "radar", "leverage", "seamless", "streamline", "unlock".
- Write from the person whose profile sends the messages.
- Explain a fact before referring to it in a later message; not every prospect gets every version.
- LinkedIn: conversational, easy-to-answer questions. Email: an intro of who we are and why we're writing, then a direct ask (call or free offer). Nobody wants to chat over email.

## Truth
- Every claim, statistic and client example must match the ICP research or an official source. Nothing invented.
- Check each message against the research before delivering it.

## Variables
- Campaigns are automated, so [First Name], {company name} and similar variables are fine where they help.
- Avoid variables that need manual research per prospect (e.g. app update dates) unless the team confirms that data is collected.
